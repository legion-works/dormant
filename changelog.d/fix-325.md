Detail: Display-switch hooks now have a total execution ceiling per switch, configurable as `displays.<id>.hooks.timeout` and defaulting to 90 seconds. A slot whose hooks exceeded it previously had no daemon-side bound at all, so a hanging hook could outlast the client waiting on the switch. Expiry is treated as hook failure, leaving the existing `abort_on_failure` setting to decide whether the switch proceeds.
