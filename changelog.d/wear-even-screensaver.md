readme_bullet: "**Wear-even screensavers** — order media and bias bounded pixel shifts toward cooler estimated panel regions"
---
User can now: configure screensaver ordering and pixel shifts to spread estimated OLED wear more evenly.

Detail: `order = "wear-even"` uses host-side luma grids and local wear heat; missing data fails open to the configured order or uniform attribution. Black overlays remain zero-wear and pixel-shift bounds stay configured.
